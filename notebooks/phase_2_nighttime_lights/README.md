# Phase 2 Notebooks: Nighttime Lights (`notebooks/phase_2_nighttime_lights/`)

This directory will contain the notebooks for **Phase 2: Nighttime Light Ingestion, Inter-Sensor Harmonization, and Gridded Processing**.

## Planned Workflows (Phase 2)

- **`01_data_source_investigation.ipynb`**: Detailed investigation of sensor calibration and overpass timing between DMSP-OLS (~21:00) and VIIRS-DNB (~01:30).
- **`02_data_collection.ipynb`**: Download and spatial subsetting of the harmonized 1992–2021 DMSP-VIIRS series and modern VIIRS-DNB composites for our 3 study windows.
- **`03_data_inspection.ipynb`**: Inspection of raster formats, cloud-free coverage bands, and radiance distributions.
- **`04_data_quality_checks.ipynb`**: Cloud-mask audits, ephemeral light filtering, and sensor saturation analysis in central Bengaluru.
- **`05_data_preprocessing.ipynb`**: Zonal statistics aggregation of annual and monthly nightlight radiances to the 300 5 km × 5 km grid cells.
- **`06_regional_visualisation.ipynb`**: Longitudinal ALAN trend maps, time-series radiance profiles, and cross-landscape comparisons.

*Note: Phase 2 will begin only after Phase 1 approval.*
